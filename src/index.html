Audio setup

Compass Soundboard plays every sound to two places at once: your headphones (so you hear it) and your stream output. To get sounds into OBS / Discord / games, install a free virtual cable (e.g. VB-Cable), set Stream output to “CABLE Input”, then use “CABLE Output” as a mic or audio source.

Like Voicemod: your mic and the sounds go out together on one device. Your mic is never sent to your headphones (no echo).
